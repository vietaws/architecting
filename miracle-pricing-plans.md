# Miracle Technologies — Pricing & Plans (FY 2026)

> **Note:** This document is a demo artifact used to show the Retrieval-Augmented
> Generation ingestion loop. It is intentionally **not** part of the main company
> profile, so the chatbot cannot answer pricing questions until this file is uploaded to

All prices are in **USD** and reflect list pricing for FY 2026. Volume discounts and
multi-year commitments are negotiated separately through the sales team.

---

## MiracleConnect — API Integration Platform

Billed monthly per environment. Each plan includes a fixed number of integration
connectors and a monthly API call allowance.

| Plan | Price / month | Connectors included | API calls / month | Support |
|------|--------------|---------------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Growth** | $1,500 | Up to 5 | 1,000,000 | Business hours (email) |
| **Scale** | $4,500 | Up to 20 | 10,000,000 | 24/5 (email + chat) |
| **Enterprise** | $12,000 | Unlimited | 50,000,000 | 24/7 (dedicated TAM) |

- **Overage rate:** $0.50 per additional 1,000 API calls beyond the plan allowance.
- **Additional connector (Growth/Scale):** $250 per connector per month.
- **Sandbox environment:** included free with every plan.

## MiracleVault — Secure Data Exchange

## MiracleFlow — Loan Origination Automation

Billed per completed loan application processed (no monthly platform fee on the Starter
tier).

| Plan | Platform fee / month | Price per processed application |
|------|---------------------|---------------------------------|
| **Starter** | $0 | $3.50 |
| **Business** | $2,500 | $2.00 |
| **Enterprise** | $8,000 | $1.00 |

- KYC/AML provider pass-through costs are billed at cost with no markup.

## MiracleInsight — Analytics & Reporting

MiracleInsight is included **free** with any paid MiracleConnect plan. Standalone
licensing is available at **$1,000 / month** per 10 named users.

- **Advisory / architecture review:** $2,500 / day.

## Billing, Discounts & Terms

- **Billing cycle:** monthly in arrears; annual pre-pay available.
- **Annual commitment discount:** 15% off list price when paid annually.
- **Multi-year discount:** additional 5% for a 2-year term, 10% for a 3-year term.
- **Non-profit / startup program:** eligible companies receive 50% off the Growth plan
  for the first 12 months.
- **Currency:** invoiced in USD; SGD and VND invoicing available for regional customers.
- **Free trial:** 30-day free trial of the MiracleConnect Growth plan, no credit card
  required.

## Pricing FAQ

**Q: How much does MiracleConnect cost per month?**
A: MiracleConnect starts at $1,500/month for the Growth plan, $4,500/month for Scale,
and $12,000/month for Enterprise.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes — a 30-day free trial of the MiracleConnect Growth plan, with no credit card
required.

**Q: Which plan includes 24/7 support?**
A: The MiracleConnect Enterprise plan includes 24/7 support with a dedicated Technical
Account Manager (TAM).

**Q: What is the API overage rate?**
A: $0.50 per additional 1,000 API calls beyond your plan's monthly allowance.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for paying annually?**
A: Yes — 15% off list price for annual pre-pay, with additional discounts for 2-year
(5%) and 3-year (10%) commitments.

**Q: How is MiracleFlow priced?**
A: Per completed loan application: $3.50 on Starter (no platform fee), $2.00 on Business
($2,500/month platform fee), and $1.00 on Enterprise ($8,000/month platform fee).
